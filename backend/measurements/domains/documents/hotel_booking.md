# Hotel Website and Property Management — Requirement Document

Version 1.0 · Synthetic measurement document for Aakalan (not a real tender)

## 1. About the hotels

1.1 Hill Crest Resorts runs three resorts in Munnar, Coorg and Ooty with 140 rooms in all.
1.2 Bookings come through travel websites and phone calls; the front desk uses a spreadsheet. The group wants its own booking website and a system for the front desk.

## 2. Users

2.1 Guests, front-desk staff, housekeeping staff, resort managers and the group's sales team will use the system.

<!-- page -->

## 3. Functional requirements

3.1 The group shall have a public website with each resort's rooms, photos, offers and a blog, which the sales team updates without a developer.
3.2 Guests shall check availability and book rooms online for chosen dates and room types.
3.3 Guests shall pay the full amount or an advance online when booking.
3.4 Guests shall get an email and WhatsApp confirmation, and a reminder two days before arrival.
3.5 Rates and available rooms shall stay in step with the travel websites (Booking.com, MakeMyTrip) through a channel manager's API.
3.6 Front-desk staff shall check guests in and out, assign rooms, and record guest ID details.
3.7 Room charges, restaurant bills and extra services shall be added to the guest's bill; the final invoice shall show GST.
3.8 Housekeeping staff shall see which rooms to clean and mark them ready from a tablet in the browser.
3.9 Resort managers shall track linen and toiletries stock and get an alert when stock is low.
3.10 The sales team shall keep guest profiles, preferences and past stays, and send offers to past guests.
3.11 Managers shall see occupancy, average room rate and revenue reports for each resort.
3.12 The website shall be found easily on Google.
3.13 The website shall be available in English and Malayalam.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 A mobile app is not required.
4.2 The restaurant's own billing machine is not part of this work; restaurant bills are entered by the front desk.

## 5. Support

5.1 The vendor shall host the website and support the system for two years.
