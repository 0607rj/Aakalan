# School Management System — Requirement Document

Version 1.0 · Synthetic measurement document for Aakalan (not a real tender)

## 1. About the schools

1.1 Vidya Bharati Trust runs four CBSE schools in Indore with about 6,000 students from nursery to class 12.
1.2 Admissions, fees, attendance and marks are kept in registers and a desktop fees program. The trust wants one system for the office, teachers and parents.

## 2. Users

2.1 Parents, students, teachers, class teachers, the accounts office, principals and the trust administrator will use the system.

<!-- page -->

## 3. Functional requirements

3.1 Parents shall apply for admission online, filling the student's details and uploading the birth certificate and photo.
3.2 Admission applications shall be checked by the admission office and approved by the principal.
3.3 Parents shall pay school fees online by UPI, card or net banking, and download receipts.
3.4 The accounts office shall set fee structures per class, apply concessions and late fines, and see pending dues.
3.5 Teachers shall mark daily attendance class-wise; parents of absent students shall get an SMS the same morning.
3.6 Teachers shall enter marks for each exam; the system shall produce CBSE-format report cards as PDF.
3.7 Teachers shall post homework and assignments with files; students shall submit their work online.
3.8 The office shall prepare the class timetable and teachers' timetables.
3.9 Parents shall use an Android and iPhone app to see attendance, homework, fees and report cards, and get notices.
3.10 Notices and circulars shall be sent to parents of a class or the whole school by app notification and SMS.
3.11 Principals shall see dashboards of attendance, fee collection and exam results across the four schools.
3.12 Student records of the last three years shall be moved from the existing fees program.
3.13 The parent app and SMS shall be available in Hindi and English.

## 4. Exclusions

4.1 School bus tracking is planned for next year and is not part of this work.
4.2 Online classes are not required; teachers use Google Meet.
4.3 Payroll for staff stays with the trust's existing accountant.

## 5. Support

5.1 The vendor shall support the system for three years after go-live.
