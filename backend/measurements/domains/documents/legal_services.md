# Legal Services Platform — Requirement Document

Version 1.0 · Synthetic measurement document for Aakalan (not a real tender)

## 1. Product

1.1 NyayMitra is a start-up that connects people and small businesses with verified lawyers across India, and gives lawyers simple tools to run their practice.
1.2 The first release covers the marketplace and the lawyers' practice tools on the web.

## 2. Users

2.1 Clients, lawyers, law-firm staff and the NyayMitra operations team will use the platform.

<!-- page -->

## 3. Functional requirements

3.1 Lawyers shall join the platform, list their practice areas, courts, languages and fees, and upload their Bar Council certificate.
3.2 The operations team shall verify each lawyer's certificate before the profile goes live.
3.3 Clients shall search lawyers by city, practice area, court and language, and read ratings and reviews.
3.4 Clients shall book a consultation slot with a lawyer.
3.5 Clients and lawyers shall message each other on the platform and share documents.
3.6 Lawyers shall keep case files with parties, court, case number, documents and notes.
3.7 Lawyers shall keep a diary of hearing dates; lawyers and clients shall get an SMS a day before each hearing.
3.8 Lawyers shall record time spent on each case and raise invoices with GST to their clients.
3.9 Clients pay lawyers directly; the platform does not collect money and charges lawyers a monthly listing fee by invoice.
3.10 The platform shall be available in English, Hindi and Tamil.
3.11 The operations team shall see reports of lawyers joined, consultations booked and reviews.

## 4. Later phases

4.1 An AI assistant that drafts legal notices is planned for a later phase.
4.2 Mobile apps will follow after the web release.

## 5. Support

5.1 The vendor shall support the platform for one year after launch.
