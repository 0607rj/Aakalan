# Video Meeting Platform — Requirement Document

Version 1.0 · Synthetic measurement document for Aakalan (not a real tender)

## 1. Product

1.1 MeetSpace is a start-up building a video meeting service for small companies and coaching institutes in India, sold as monthly plans.
1.2 The first release must let a host run a meeting of up to 100 people from a browser or a phone.

## 2. Users

2.1 Hosts, participants, company administrators and the MeetSpace support team will use the product.

<!-- page -->

## 3. Functional requirements

3.1 Hosts shall schedule a meeting with a date, time and agenda and invite people by email; invitees get a calendar invite.
3.2 Participants shall join from a link in the browser without installing anything, or from the Android and iOS apps.
3.3 Meetings shall carry live video and audio for up to 100 participants, with a gallery view and the active speaker shown large.
3.4 Participants shall share their screen.
3.5 Participants shall chat with everyone or privately during the meeting.
3.6 Hosts shall use a waiting room, mute or remove participants, and lock the meeting.
3.7 Hosts shall record a meeting; recordings shall be stored in the cloud for 30 days and shared by link.
3.8 The system shall show live captions in English while people speak.
3.9 Companies shall buy a monthly or yearly plan and pay by card or UPI; plans renew automatically.
3.10 Company administrators shall add and remove their staff and see usage reports: meetings held, minutes used and recordings stored.
3.11 Employees of companies on the business plan shall sign in with their company's Google Workspace or Microsoft account.

<!-- page -->

## 4. Non-functional requirements and exclusions

4.1 Video shall work on a 2 Mbps connection and adjust quality to the network.
4.2 Calls shall be encrypted in transit.
4.3 Webinars for more than 100 people and live streaming to YouTube are planned for a later phase.
4.4 An AI meeting summary is not required in this release.

## 5. Support

5.1 The vendor shall support the product for one year after launch.
