# Beekeepers' Co-operative Management System — Requirement Document

Version 1.0 · Synthetic measurement document for Aakalan (not a real tender)

## 1. About the co-operative

1.1 Madhu Sahakari is a co-operative of about 2,500 beekeepers in the Western Ghats of Maharashtra and Karnataka. It collects their honey, tests and bottles it, and sells it under its own brand.
1.2 Records are kept in registers at 12 collection centres. The co-operative wants one system for members, centres and the head office.

## 2. Users

2.1 Member beekeepers, collection centre staff, quality testers, the accounts team and head office managers will use the system.

## 3. Functional requirements

3.1 Beekeepers shall register as members online with their village, bank details and the number of hives they keep.
3.2 Members shall keep a record of each apiary: its location, hives, queen replacement dates and inspection notes.
3.3 Head office shall see all apiaries on a map.
3.4 At the collection centre, staff shall weigh each member's honey, record moisture and grade, and print a label with a QR code for each drum.
3.5 Centre staff shall scan the drum QR codes with the tablet camera when drums leave for the processing plant.
3.6 Collection centres often lose internet; entries shall be saved on the tablet and sent when the connection returns.
3.7 Centre staff use the system in a browser on tablets; a separate mobile app is not required.
3.8 Quality testers shall record lab results for each batch, and every jar shall be traceable back to the drums and members it came from.
3.9 The accounts team shall work out each member's payment from the weight and grade, and send payments to members' bank accounts in bulk.
3.10 Members shall get an SMS when their honey is weighed and when payment is sent.
3.11 Screens shall be available in Marathi, Kannada and English.
3.12 The co-operative shall sell bottled honey to the public in an online shop with a cart and online payment.
3.13 Managers shall see reports of honey collected by centre, by month and by grade, and payments made.

## 4. Later phases

4.1 Recognising bee diseases from photos of the hives is planned for a later phase.

## 5. Support

5.1 The vendor shall support the system for two years after go-live.
